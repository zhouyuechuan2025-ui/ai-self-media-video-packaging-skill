# Visual structures

| Structure | Best use | Default safe zone |
|---|---|---|
| impact-question | direct question or sharp tension | center |
| contrarian-stamp | correction or counterintuitive conclusion | center |
| gradient-keyword | one dominant term | center |
| split-conflict | two opposing states | full |
| three-beat-hook | result, tension, promise | center |
| side-insight-card | supporting explanation beside a face | left |
| dual-concept | two related concepts | full |
| keyword-relay | short chain of concepts | center |
| adaptive-steps | ordered execution | right |
| signal-route | flow, system, or connection | full |
| state-switch | before/after state change | right |
| chapter-timeline | progression across a section | full |
| evidence-pip | supporting proof beside source footage | left |
| evidence-takeover | proof requiring focused reading | full |
| metric-counter | one source-backed or attributed number | center |
| before-after | clear comparison | full |
| capability-matrix | multiple capabilities or dimensions | full |
| completion-rail | conclusion and next action | center |

Do not select a structure solely for variety. The spoken logic, protected screen region, and evidence status decide the structure.
